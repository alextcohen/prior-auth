Synthetic prior-authorization fixtures
=====================================

Every patient, practice, member ID, and payer in this folder is fictional.
The PDFs are digital text, in the same general shape as the assignment samples:
a coverage guideline with a position statement, AND/OR criteria, a coding table,
and a chart with demographics, problems, visits, a diagnostic report, and a
planned procedure that names a CPT or HCPCS code.

They extend the plan's decision cases. The assignment pair is still the one
outside this folder:

  Guideline: Documents/guideline.pdf
             BCBS Florida 02-33000-31, varicose veins.
             Compression trial is 3 months. CPT 36473 (mechanochemical
             ablation) is investigational. CPT 36468 (spider veins) is
             non-covered. Perforator ablation in the same session as
             saphenous treatment is excluded.
  Chart:     Documents/robert_mitchell_chart_final.pdf
             CPT 33207 pacemaker. Expected packet: Do not submit.
             That code is not in the vein policy.

Packet labels below are the three outcomes from the plan:
  Ready for review     every required group is supported by the chart
  Fix before submit    the procedure matches, but a required fact is missing
                       or the trial is too short to satisfy the policy
  Do not submit        the code is outside the policy, on the investigational
                       or non-covered list, or a hard exclusion applies

The answer key is only in this file. It is not written into the PDFs.


Guidelines in this folder
-------------------------

guideline_harborlight_varicose_veins_HHP-VEN-2025-04.pdf
  Fictional Harborlight Health Plan vein policy.
  Same shape as the BCBS vein policy, with two deliberate differences:
  the symptom-plus-compression indication requires 6 consecutive weeks,
  not 3 months, and mechanochemical ablation (36473, 36474) is covered.
  Spider veins (36468) stay non-covered. Same-session perforator ablation
  stays excluded.

guideline_palm_mutual_pad_PM-02-41000-18.pdf
  Fictional Palm Mutual policy for lower-extremity stents.
  Covered: iliac stent 37221, femoropopliteal stent 37226.
  Investigational: intravascular lithotripsy C9764 and C9765.
  A femoropopliteal stent needs a stenosis of at least 70 percent, an
  ABI of 0.90 or lower (or TBI 0.70 or lower), AND either critical limb
  ischemia OR lifestyle-limiting claudication after a 12-week walking
  program. Critical limb ischemia does not require the walking trial.

guideline_palm_mutual_carotid_PM-02-28000-09.pdf
  Fictional Palm Mutual policy for extracranial carotid revascularization.
  Covered: stent with distal embolic protection 37215, endarterectomy 35301.
  Investigational: stent without embolic protection 37216.
  Both covered pathways require an ipsilateral TIA, amaurosis fugax, or
  nondisabling stroke within 6 months, plus NASCET stenosis of at least
  70 percent on imaging within 6 months. Asymptomatic stenosis is excluded.
